# Customer Feedback Analysis: Estates & Facilities

Analysis of customer satisfaction feedback for a university Estates & Facilities team, prepared as part of a **Data and Customer Insight Officer** interview task.

**Author:** Mohammed Afzal
**Date:** June 2024

## Project overview

The goal was to understand how satisfied customers are with work carried out by Estates & Facilities, identify the strongest and weakest service areas, and recommend how to improve customer service.

## Repository contents

| File | Description |
|------|-------------|
| `Customer_feedback_data.xlsx` | Mock survey responses (work location, type of work, satisfaction, commitment rating, free-text feedback, likelihood to recommend) |
| `Customer_satisfaction_presentation.pptx` | Presentation of the data, insights and recommendations |
| `README.md` | This file |

## About the data

Each record is a piece of feedback on completed work. Key fields:

- **Where was the work located?** (8 sites, mainly Strand, Guy's and Denmark Hill campuses)
- **Type of work** (Hard Services, Soft Services, Cleaning, Security, Residences)
- **Satisfied with time taken?** (Yes/No)
- **Team commitment rating** (1-5)
- **Likelihood to recommend** (0-10, used to calculate NPS)
- **Free-text feedback**

## Key findings

- **82%** of respondents were satisfied with the time taken to complete work.
- **83%** are likely to recommend the service to others.
- **69%** gave a 5/5 rating for the team's commitment to outstanding service.
- **NPS of 56%** overall, with large variation between services.
- **Soft Services** performed strongly (93% gave a 5 rating), while **Hard Services** (lighting, plumbing, decorating, heating/cooling) showed the most dissatisfaction (59% gave a 5 rating, 16% gave a 1).
- Around 83% of work falls under Hard and Soft Services, so results for other services (e.g. only 1 Security response) should be treated with caution.

## Recommendations

1. **Balance the data**: collect a more even spread of feedback across locations and types of work.
2. **Focus on Hard Services**: this is where improvement will have the greatest impact.
3. **Capture who did the work** (staff/team/department) so poor performance or good practice can be pinpointed.
4. **Improve ticket closure communication**: free-text comments repeatedly ask for updates during the job and a description of what was done when the ticket closes.
